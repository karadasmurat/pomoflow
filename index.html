Ways of working

Four ways.
Your way.

Some days you just show up. Some days you plan weeks ahead. Some days you're somewhere in between.

shwn. works your way, and all of it counts.

The Sprinter
Just show up

No setup. No planning. Opens the app and starts. The commitment is already made — in their head, before the app knows anything. shwn. just witnesses it. Records it honestly. Some days this is enough. Some days this is everything.

You showed up. That's the whole thing.

The Namer
Ten seconds of intention

Takes ten seconds before starting. Picks a focus area. "This one's for the thesis." That single choice — made before the work begins — changes what the session feels like. It belongs somewhere now. At the end of the week, shwn. shows exactly where the time went.

Ten seconds of intention. Everything after is clearer.

The Planner
Give the deadline a shape

Sees the deadline and gives it a shape. Twelve sessions, four days, Friday at 17:00. Each one placed where it can actually happen. Now the deadline isn't a date looming somewhere — it's a path with steps already committed to. They know exactly where they stand, any day they check.

The deadline is still there. Its shadow isn't.

The Reflector
Look back honestly

Looks back honestly. What did I give this week? Does it match what I said mattered? Not to judge — to understand. One honest look changes the next week. And the one after that.

Is your time reflecting your priorities?

The feeling you know

That dread before a deadline.
It lives in the dark.

A deadline doesn't just stay in your to-do list. It moves into your head, opening a new tab. Putting it in your calendar won't close it.

Here's the cruel part: the avoidance isn't rest. Every time you try to relax, your brain keeps scanning, keeps reminding, keeps exhausting you. Even when you aren't thinking about it, a part of you is quietly dedicated to tracking it. This is why you feel tired even on days you didn't do anything.

Here's the good part: you don't have to finish it to feel better. You don't need a fat tick in your to-do list. You just need to start moving.

Your brain opens tabs.
shwn. helps you close them.
