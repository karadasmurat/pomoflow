The loop

Four steps.
At your pace.

Progress isn't built in marathons. It's built in honest, repeated acts of showing up. shwn. let's do it again. better.

01 — Define
Name what matters

Define what deserves your attention. Add the areas of work, study, or life you want to invest in and give each a color. These aren't just labels — they're a declaration of what you're choosing to show up for.

02 — Commit
Set your intent

Before the timer starts, name where this block of time is going. That single conscious choice — made before the work begins — is where busy becomes purposeful.

03 — Focus
Show up fully

Work. Rest. Repeat. Your only job is to be present for the session you committed to. Progress is built in honest, repeated acts of showing up.

04 — Reflect
See where you went

Review how your effort is flowing across focus areas — not to judge, but to understand. One honest look is worth more than a week of good intentions.

The feeling you know

That dread before a deadline.
It lives in the dark.

Not the night-before panic. The weeks-before weight. You know the deadline is there. It's in your calendar. You're aware of it. But awareness isn't a plan — and a plan you haven't committed to doesn't move you.

Plan. Break it into sessions you'll actually give. Put them on the calendar before the week starts. Now the deadline has a path.

Focus. Show up for each session. Your only job is to be present. The record builds session by session.

Reflect. See what you've given and what remains. Not to judge — to understand. The dread has less to hold onto when the path is visible.

A committed session is worth more
than a deadline you're aware of.

shwn. gives you all three. The dread doesn't disappear. But it stops living in the dark.
