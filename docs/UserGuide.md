# PomoFlow User Guide

---

## What PomoFlow is

Most productivity tools measure one thing: whether you finished.

PomoFlow measures something different — whether you showed up. Instead of tracking task completion, it tracks the effort you invest across the areas of work, study, and life that matter to you. Every session you complete is proof of progress, regardless of where the finish line sits.

The result is a different relationship with your time. Not guilt when things aren't done, but clarity about where your energy is actually going — and whether it reflects what you care about.

PomoFlow is built around a simple loop you repeat daily: **Define** what deserves your attention. **Commit** to where a block of time is going before you start. **Focus** by showing up fully for each session. **Reflect** on how your effort is flowing across your areas.

The sections below follow that loop — from your first session to planning your week to understanding your progress over time.

---

## Your first session

The fastest way to understand PomoFlow is to run a session.

**1. Create a Focus Area.** Open the Focus Areas panel (`G`) and add your first area — something you want to invest time in today. Give it a name and a color.

**2. Set your intent.** On the main timer screen, tap *What are you focusing on?* and select the area you just created. This single step is where busy becomes purposeful — you're naming, before the work begins, where this time is going.

**3. Start the timer.** Press `Space` or tap Start. A 25-minute focus session begins. Your only job is to be present for it.

**4. Take your break.** When the session ends, the timer holds at 0:00. PomoFlow waits for you to choose when to continue — it won't rush you into your break. Press `Space` when you're ready.

**5. Repeat.** After four focus sessions, a longer break is triggered. That's one full Pomodoro cycle.

When you're done, your time is logged against your Focus Area and appears in your effort history. That's the foundation everything else builds on.

## The timer

### Modes

| Mode | Default | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Focus | 25 minutes | Deep work dedicated to a Focus Area |
| Short Break | 5 minutes | A brief pause to reset between sessions |
| Long Break | 15 minutes | Extended rest after every 4 focus sessions |

Switch modes manually at any time using `1`, `2`, or `3`.

### Accuracy

The timer uses wall-clock accuracy — it tracks your session's target end-time rather than counting down in the foreground. This means sessions stay precise even if your browser tab moves to the background or your device sleeps briefly.

### Adjusting durations

Open the Orbit menu (the timer icon) to set durations quickly in preset increments between 10 and 60 minutes. Use the `+` and `−` buttons for fine-tuning. Changes take effect at the start of your next session.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Start / pause |
| `R` | Reset timer |
| `N` | Skip to next session |

## Focus Areas

Focus Areas are the categories you invest time in — work, research, creative projects, health, or anything else that deserves your attention. They're not just labels. They're a declaration of what you're choosing to show up for.

### Creating and editing areas

Open the Focus Areas panel with `G`. Add an area, give it a name, choose a color and an icon. There's no limit to how many you can create, but most people find that four to six areas covers everything that genuinely matters.

### Attributing sessions

Before each session, select the area you're working in. If you forget, you can edit the attribution from the Activity Log after the fact.

### XP and levels

Every minute of focused time earns XP. Your rank advances as you build consistency — from Novice upward through higher tiers. This is a reflection of your investment over time, not a productivity score.

## The Focus Planner

The Focus Planner is where you move from reactive sessions to intentional weeks. Open it with `P`.

It's a weekly calendar where you place *commitment blocks* — planned effort against your Focus Areas — and *deadline markers* — real-world moments you're working toward. Completed sessions automatically appear as a second layer, so you can see planned effort and actual effort side by side.

### Commitment blocks

A commitment block is an intention: *I will give this Focus Area N sessions on this day.*

**On desktop**, drag a Focus Area card from the sidebar and drop it onto the day and time you want. A popover appears asking for session count and an optional label. Drag the bottom edge of a block to adjust the session count after placement.

**On mobile**, tap any day column to open the day drawer, then tap an empty slot or the `+` button. Choose your Focus Area, set a start time, and use the `+` / `−` stepper to set the number of sessions. The derived end time updates live as you adjust.

Block duration is calculated from your session settings — session length, short break, and long break — so it always reflects how long that number of sessions will actually take.

### Deadline markers

A deadline marker is an event in the world — a submission, a presentation, a moment you're planning toward. It lives at the top of its day column and can be linked to one or more Focus Areas, or to none at all.

Add a deadline from the `+` button in the header. Give it a name, a date, and optionally attach it to the Focus Areas it relates to.

Deadline markers never turn red as the date approaches. The calendar is not here to generate urgency — it's here to give you clarity.

### Planned vs. actual

The calendar shows two layers simultaneously: your commitment blocks (what you planned) and your completed session blocks (what actually happened). Use the filter control to view both together, planned only, or actual only.

The gap between planned and actual is information, not failure. Drift is normal. The value is in being able to see it clearly.

## Seeing your progress

### The Dashboard

The Dashboard gives you a high-level view of how your effort has been distributed — a visual breakdown across Focus Areas, total focus time, session count, your current day streak, and a full Activity Log with every session. You can edit session durations or delete entries from the log if needed.

### Logical day rollover

PomoFlow uses a 4:00 AM rollover for its daily calculations. Sessions completed before 4 AM are attributed to the previous calendar date, so late-night work lands in the right day's totals.

### Achievements

| Badge | Condition |
|---|---|
| 🌱 First Steps | Complete your first focus session |
| 🔥 Habitual | Maintain a 3-day focus streak |
| 🌊 Deep Diver | Reach 10 total hours of focus |
| 📐 Architect | Reach 10 unique focus targets |
| 📤 Socialite | Share your progress |
| 👑 Unstoppable | Reach 100 total hours of focus |
| 🦉 Night Owl | Complete a session between midnight and 4 AM *(hidden)* |
| 🌅 Early Bird | Start a session before 6 AM *(hidden)* |

## Your profile

### Persona

Your chosen Persona appears as an avatar in the header, alongside your Rank and XP, and stays visible across the app. It's not a fixed identity — it's a reflection of how you're showing up right now.

To change your persona, open your Profile, click the **edit icon** on your avatar, and select a new one from the gallery. Use the **Share** button to post your current "Focus Mood" to X.

### Rank and XP

Your Rank and XP accumulate automatically as you log focused time. They're displayed in the header next to your Persona and updated in your Profile. Like your effort history, they're a record of investment over time — not a performance target.

## Ambient music

PomoFlow includes a built-in ambient music player. The widget sits below the timer controls and streams audio tracks in the background while you work.

- Use **prev / next** to cycle through tracks, or tap the play button to start the current one
- The disc animates while music is playing
- Enable **Pause on break** in Settings → Time to automatically stop music during break sessions and resume when your next focus session begins
- Track selection persists across sessions

---

## Customization

**Session durations.** Adjust focus, short break, and long break durations in Settings. Toggle auto-start if you prefer the timer to move automatically between phases rather than waiting for you.

**Visual styles.** Switch between light and dark themes using the toggle in the sidenav.

**Sharing.** Customize templates for posting your focus status to X or other platforms, in Settings under Sharing.

## Reference

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Start / pause timer |
| `R` | Reset timer |
| `N` | Skip to next session |
| `G` | Toggle Focus Areas panel |
| `P` | Toggle Focus Planner |
| `1` | Switch to Focus mode |
| `2` | Switch to Short Break mode |
| `3` | Switch to Long Break mode |

### Privacy and data

All data is stored locally in your browser first. If you sign in, your sessions, focus areas, planned blocks, and paths sync to Supabase for cross-device access — settings and profile stay local. To manually back up or migrate your history, use the Export and Import tools in Settings → Data. Clearing your browser's site data will reset local history, so export or sign in regularly if your history matters to you.

### Technical requirements

- **Browser:** Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (current versions)
- **Notifications:** Enable browser notifications in Settings to receive alerts when sessions end
- **Storage:** Local storage must be enabled for PomoFlow to save your data
